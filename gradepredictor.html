How the course grade is calculated

Your three tests are ranked from highest to lowest. The best test counts more than the others.

  • In-class work: 10%
  • Homework: 10%
  • Labs: 20%
  • Best test: 30%
  • Second-best test: 20%
  • Lowest test: 10%

Letter grades: A is 89.5 or higher, B+ is 84.5 to 89.4, B is 79.5 to 84.4, C+ is 74.5 to 79.4, C is 69.5 to 74.4, D+ is 64.5 to 69.4, D is 59.5 to 64.4, and F is below 59.5.
